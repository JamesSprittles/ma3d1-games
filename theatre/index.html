You Are the Fluid

The lecture theatre is a channel of fluid and every row of seats is one layer of it. The front wall is the lecturer's bench. Your phone is your row: it shows how fast your layer is moving, lights up and beeps when the motion reaches you. Everyone's phone runs the same exact solution on the same clock, so the whole room shows \(u(y,t)\).
